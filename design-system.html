5. Page Layout

Every topic page follows the same vertical structure.

1.   topnav  — sticky white bar · hamburger and brand left · centered search · About and settings gear right
2.   header  — eyeline · h1 · tagline · subtitle · soft gradient from --header-grad to --bg
3.   law-feature or main.grid  — formula banner (if any) full width, then Origin and Law columns, then cards on hairline dividers
4.   section.references  — Further Reading · 1–2 col grid of ref rows
5.   fat footer  — black · brand + blurb + social | Site links | Sister Sites · copyright bar beneath

Header spacing

header {
  padding: 60px 32px 28px;       /* desktop */
  background: linear-gradient(180deg, var(--header-grad) 0%, var(--bg) 100%);
}

@media (max-width: 600px) {
  header { padding: 32px 20px 28px; }
}

Card subgrid

Each card has three internal sections: header, demo, meta. By using grid-template-rows: subgrid; grid-row: span 3, header rows line up across each row of cards regardless of description length.

8. Themes

Four themes, implemented as a class on the html element (like ux-components.com): light is the default, and dark, beige, and blue re-map the surface tokens. Chosen in the settings sheet and applied on Save, persisted in localStorage as ux-theme, with an inline head script preventing a flash of the wrong theme. The Font Size setting scales the whole page (85–130%) and persists as ux-font.

9. Navigation Chrome

The topnav is white with a soft drop shadow and holds four things: the hamburger, the brand, a centered search field, and the About link with the settings gear on the right. There is no breadcrumb.

The hamburger opens a full-page panel that slides down from beneath the topnav, showing every page grouped by category in left-to-right columns on desktop and collapsing to accordions on mobile. The settings gear opens a right-anchored sheet (340px) with Theme, Language, and a Font Size slider (85–130%). Choices are staged and applied on Save; Cancel reverts, and the sheet closes only via Save, Cancel, or the X — never by clicking outside or pressing Escape. Labels are 11px uppercase; the active option row carries a left bar.

Search is a centered grey pill (44px tall, 10px radius) with a magnifier icon on the left and a clear button when text is entered — no focus outline; focus darkens the field and adds a soft halo. Results load from a generated search-index.json (one entry per page, card, glossary term, and person), rank title matches above body matches, and drop down full-width beneath the topnav with a 280ms slide animation. Focusing the empty field shows Recently viewed — the last pages visited, kept in localStorage. On mobile the field collapses to an icon; tapping it hands the whole topnav to search.

The footer is a black fat footer in two tiers. The top tier has three columns: the brand with a short blurb and social icons (LinkedIn, Medium, Substack, X, Bluesky); Site links (About, Glossary, People, Site Map); and Sister Sites (UX Components, GPT Prompt Guides, Extend Chrome). A bottom bar carries the copyright. Column headings are 14px, and every external link opens a new window. The topnav chrome, search, settings sheet, and footer are all injected by tokens.js, so pages never carry the markup.

10. Detail Page Patterns

Recurring structures on law and principle pages, all driven by shared tokens.css rules.

Formula banner — pages whose law has an equation (Fitts, Hick, Miller, Doherty, Pareto, Steering, Weber–Fechner, Power Law, Fogg) open the law-feature section with a full-width .law-formula banner: the equation at twice body size (32px, weight 400, −1px tracking) with a one-line plain-language reading beneath, above the Origin and Law columns.

Related chips — cards that overlap with other laws end with a Related row: small bordered chips linking to the sibling pages. The label is localized, and links use full-concept filenames (hicks-law.html, not hicks.html).

People links — every “Coined by” name links to its anchor on the People page (people.html#p-slug), where each person has an alphabetized entry.

Accessibility — a skip link jumps past the injected chrome, decorative illustrations are aria-hidden, search results announce through a live region, theme options use radio semantics, faint text stays at or above 4.5:1, and prefers-reduced-motion disables the draw-on and entrance animations.

11. Localization

Six languages — English, French, German, Spanish, Japanese, Chinese — selected from the settings gear and persisted as ux-lang. English pages ship with zero overhead.

For any other language, i18n.js loads lazily and applies two layers: a chrome dictionary (menus, labels, titles, the search placeholder) and a per-page content overlay from i18n/<lang>/<page>.json that translates every card, bio, definition, and meta value. Overlays apply by selector and index, and any section whose element count no longer matches falls back to English silently. Sources for the overlays live in i18n/src/ (not deployed); regenerating them after copy changes keeps translations in sync.
